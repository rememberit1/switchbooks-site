Mercury Books starts fresh. Your old books don't come with you, and QuickBooks won't keep them forever.

Mercury Books doesn't import your history

Past transactions and categorization rules from QuickBooks Online or Xero don't come across. Mercury recommends a clean cutover with opening balances, and you keep the old records somewhere else.

Source: Mercury Help Center ↗
⏳

QuickBooks deletes your data a year after you cancel

After you cancel QuickBooks Online you get read-only access for one year. After that the data is gone, which is a problem when your accountant, a lender or an auditor asks for prior years.

Source: QuickBooks Help ↗

FAQ

Don't see your question? Ask it on the scoping call.

Does Mercury Books really not import my QuickBooks history?

Right. Mercury's help center says Books doesn't import transaction history from QuickBooks Online, Xero or NetSuite, and recommends a clean cutover with opening balances. We do that cutover for you and archive the history so it isn't lost.

Do you support Xero too?

Yes. The move works the same way from Xero: chart of accounts, opening balances, open invoices, and an optional archive of your Xero history and attachments.

What do you need from me?

Access to your QuickBooks or Xero file (an accountant or user invite works), access to Mercury Books, and a cutover date. We send a short checklist after the scoping call.

When does the 48 hours start?

Once we have access to both systems and you've confirmed your cutover date. Larger or messier files may take longer, and we'll tell you before we start.

Will you change or cancel my QuickBooks?

No. We only read your data. You decide when to cancel, and we suggest doing it after you've checked the move and have your archive.

What's in the ExitVault archive?

Your general ledger, yearly profit & loss and balance sheet reports, customer and vendor lists, and your receipts and attachments, organized and searchable. It's yours to keep and download.

Is SwitchBooks part of Mercury or Intuit?

No. SwitchBooks is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Mercury or Intuit.

What if Mercury Books isn't right for my business?

We'll tell you on the free call. For example, Books is USD-only, so multi-currency businesses may not be a fit yet.
